4. Convert these px values to rem: 8px, 24px, 32px, 40px (base 16px).

answers:  base px=16px  (pixel value divided by 16)

8px :
   8/16 = 0.5 rem

24px:
   24/16 =1.5 rem

32px:
   32/16 = 2 rem

40px:
   40/16 =2.5 rem
